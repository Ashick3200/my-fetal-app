My Fetal – Privacy Policy

Effective date: 7 November 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how the My Fetal mobile application (“My Fetal”, “we”, “our” or “the app”) handles information.

1. Information you enter

My Fetal lets you enter information such as a mother's name, last menstrual period (LMP) date, pregnancy type (single or twins), and a heartbeat value. These details are used to display pregnancy-week information and the app's illustrative baby-journey content.

2. Where information is stored

The information you enter is stored locally on your device. According to the app's current design, it is not uploaded to a My Fetal server or an account, and we do not receive a copy of it.

3. Advertising, analytics and tracking

My Fetal does not display advertisements and does not use analytics or advertising-tracking SDKs.

4. Sharing and sale of information

We do not sell your entered information. The app's current design does not send the locally stored information to us or share it with third parties.

5. Data retention and deletion

Your entered information remains in the app's local storage on your device. You can remove it by clearing the app's storage or uninstalling the app. Uninstalling an app may not remove device-level backups managed separately by your operating system.

6. Children's privacy

My Fetal is not designed for children to use. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

7. Security

Because information is stored locally, you should protect access to your device. No method of electronic storage is guaranteed to be completely secure.

8. Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy when the app's features or data practices change. The latest version will be published at this page, with its updated effective date.

9. Contact us

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy or My Fetal's privacy practices, contact us at ashicklabs@gmail.com.
